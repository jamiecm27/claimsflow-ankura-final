Forgot your password?

For remote access or analyst accounts: ask the ClaimsFlow administrator to open Administration → Account settings and reset your account password. No Terminal changes are needed.

If you are the administrator: on the Mac mini, open Applications → ClaimsFlow v12.1 → Recover Admin Password.command. This also creates the first administrator account if needed. Safari opens a private local password-reset page.

For security, remote visitors cannot reset the administrator password without access to the Mac mini.
